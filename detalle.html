The Craftsmanship

Inspired by Brutalist architecture and the philosophy of subtractive sculpture, the Obsidian Monolith challenges the playful origins of the rubber duck. Each piece undergoes a 72-hour curing process in a specialized kiln, followed by meticulous hand-sanding by master artisans to achieve a texture that absorbs light rather than reflecting it. The result is a profound silence in sculptural form.
